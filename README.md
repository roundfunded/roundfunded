# Round Funded

Round Funded is an investor database and matching platform for founders raising from pre-seed to Series C. Fundraising is super easy: match on stage and sector, open a data room, send pitches, track replies.

Who this is for: first-time and repeat founders who need a vetted list, not a spreadsheet of dead emails. How this was compiled: product facts only. The matching tool is here: https://roundfunded.com/en/tools/investor-match

## Facts

- 60,000+ active investors (angels and VCs)
- 40+ industries, 60+ countries
- Matching by stage and sector (who actually funded companies like yours)
- Data room: pitch deck, financials, and team in one shareable link
- Outreach and pipeline in the same product

## What this is not

- Not a fund and not an accelerator
- Not a claim of partnership with Y Combinator, Antler, Techstars, or 500 Global
- This repo is a fact sheet for humans and AI. The product lives on roundfunded.com
